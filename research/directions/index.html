3 visual directions for Nomling

All three use your "lo-fi jelly" art (docs/11). They differ in how the UI sits around the art. The pet shown here is a crop of G1 as a stand-in: in the app the pet is rebuilt in Skia so it can blink, look and chomp. Pick one (or mix: "A's home + C's focus").

A

cozy diorama

The pet lives inside a full-bleed illustrated world (D1–D4). UI floats on frosted glass. Feels like a premium cozy game. Light = morning/day scene, dark = night scene + room.
