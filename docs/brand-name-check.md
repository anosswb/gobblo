# Brand name check: "Nomling"

Run on **2026-11-06** with the replica-brand skill (name only; palette and art direction stay as in `docs/02` and `docs/11`).
These are screening checks, not legal clearance. Before paying for a trademark filing or the store fees under a final name, a trademark lawyer should do a full search.

## Verdict: UNSAFE (change the name before launch)

1. **An iPhone app named "Nomling" went live 15 days ago.** "Nomling: Fold Monster" by Marvin Kicha, released 2026-10-22, bundle `com.marvinkicha.nomling`. It is a cute monster that eats snacks with a chomp sound and haptics. Same exact name, same store, and a very close idea (cute creature that eats). Users searching "Nomling" will find it first, reviews will mix up, and its owner has a prior-use claim if he files a trademark.
2. **"Nomlings" (plural) is a virtual pet that eats your finished tasks.** A Windows Tamagotchi covered by Korben on 2026-07-06: a creature that "eats" Claude Code tokens or, in companion mode, your checked-off tasks (+10 XP and a snack per task), hatches from an egg, grows antennas, can't die, and sells a cosmetic pack. That is almost our concept with an "s" added. Different platform today, but it shows the name and the idea are already in use in our exact category.
3. **Both main domains are taken by someone else** (unless Anass registered them himself, see below). `nomling.com` was registered on 2026-07-09 (3 days after the Nomlings article) and is parked. `nomling.app` was registered on 2026-08-28.
4. **@nomling is taken on TikTok and Instagram** (inactive personal accounts).
5. No trademark for NOMLING showed up in the one US mirror we could read (Trademarkia, 0 results), but the official USPTO, EUIPO and WIPO searches could not be read automatically. A clean trademark search would not fix points 1 and 2 anyway.

What would change this to RISKY: if `nomling.com` and `nomling.app` are Anass's own, and "Nomling: Fold Monster" is removed. Point 2 would still remain.

## 2. Backup names

How they were picked: 30+ ideas around eat / nom / bite / munch / gobble / start, then cut by: no app with the same name in the App Store, `.app` domain free, short, easy to say in English, French and Moroccan Arabic/Darija.

Language traps found while filtering (important for every future name):
- **"bite" is a vulgar word in French.** No Biteling, Bitebean, Firstbite, etc.
- Avoid **p** and **v** as key sounds (missing in Arabic) and long names (Crunchling was dropped for this).
- Avoid **"nom" + pet** combos (Om Nom, Nomlings, Nomling: Fold Monster already sit there).
- Old name "nibbl": already taken in the App Store (START_HERE.md). Don't go back to "nibble" names.

Every `.com` exact domain was taken for all names checked (normal for short invented words), so each one uses `name.app` or `getname.com`.

Notes on the backups:
- **Gobblo**: "Gobble" (a US meal-kit company, app `co.noquarter.gobble` on Play) and "Gobblet" (a board game) exist. Different category and spelling, but a lawyer should look at it. Still the cleanest overall.
- **Chomplet**: no app, no Trademarkia hit, TikTok free. French speakers read it "shom-pleh"; that still lands the "complet" pun. English speakers read "chomp-let". Fine both ways.
- **Yumling**: App Store has "Yumzy: Food Tracker Pet" and many "Yum..." food apps, so "Yum" is a crowded start. Least distinct of the five.
- Chomby / Chomzy: domains fine, but both handles are taken. Lower picks.

**Top 2: Gobblo, then Chomplet.**
