# 13 — Gobblo Plus: the offer (founder-offer method)

_2026-11-06. Method: the Offers lens (founder-board/lenses.md, a summary of the framework in
*$100M Offers*). Evidence: docs/07 (1,504 real reviews of Finch, Tiimo, Structured, Goblin Tools).
Panel: simulated buyers (founder-consumer, --quick, 20 each, seed 7). Simulated buyers are good at
finding objections and bad at predicting real sales: treat buy rates as an upper bound._

## Rules we can't break
- Core loop free forever (dump → bite → 2-minute start → feed). Repeat tasks, reminders, dark mode,
  loud nudges and backup stay free (docs/07: paywalled basics = 1★).
- iOS: one-time Plus, no subscription, no trial. Android: same items, earned with sparks + optional
  rewarded ads (no in-app purchases from Morocco).
- No server in v1. Everything below works offline.

## 3. The stack (final, after the panel)
- **Name:** Gobblo Plus. Three packs, or all three as Plus.
- **When:** the offer appears only after day 7 (never in week one, never in onboarding).
- **Price (iOS):** Plus **$4.99** one-time, or any single pack **$1.99**. Panel price answers: bargain
  ~$2.50, "getting expensive" ~$6, "too expensive" ~$10, so $4.99 sits in the comfortable zone and
  $6.99 is near the top of it.
- **🚀 Start Kit (the reason people pay):** lock-screen / home-screen widget with your next bite and a
  one-tap 2-minute start; the focus timer on the lock screen (Live Activity on iOS, ongoing
  notification on Android); one-tap routine packs.
- **🎩 Make It Yours:** the full Closet (hats and accessories, colours, Home backgrounds) + app themes.
- **🎧 Focus:** sound mixer (layer two soundscapes) + the weekly Brain Wrapped card.
- **Promise:** one-time, no subscription, no trial, ever (written in the terms). Every cosmetic drop
  in the first year included. Refunds through Apple, never fought.
- **True urgency:** launch-month founding-member badge (gold antenna). Real, and it ends.
- **Android:** each pack costs sparks (120–150 ✦, about 2–3 weeks of normal use); optional rewarded
  ads speed it up. Nothing is locked forever behind money on Android.

## 5. Panel re-test (simulated buyers, 20 each, seed 7, same people)
| | old plan (docs/09: $6.99, species, room, widgets, iCloud…) | new stack (above) |
|---|---|---|
| buy | **5 / 20 (25%)** | **11 / 20 (55%)** |
| who buys | collectors only | collectors, sharers, tool optimisers, half of review-readers |
| median "bargain" price | $2.99 | $2.49 |
| median "too expensive" | $8.00 | $9.99 |

What moved:
- **"It's only cosmetics"** (top reason to pass in v1) dropped: buyers named the **lock-screen widget +
  one-tap 2-minute start** as the thing worth paying for.
- **"I'll quit in two weeks"** softened by "offer only after day 7" and the $1.99 single packs.
- **"Will it become a subscription?"** answered by the written promise (still the top doubt for the
  "burned by subscriptions" group: 1 of 4 buys).
- Still no: free-forever users (0 of 3) and broke students. Fine: the free app is for them.

Honest limit: these are simulated buyers. They are good at finding objections, bad at predicting real
sales; treat 55% as an upper bound. Confirm with real users (TestFlight / Play testers, a price test
in RevenueCat) before launch.

## 6. Build order
1. **Plus foundation:** owned packs, spark purchase on Android, the Plus screen (offer after day 7).
2. **Start Kit:** routine packs → Android widget + ongoing focus notification → iOS widget + Live
   Activity (needs Xcode 26 / EAS iOS build).
3. **Make It Yours:** hats on the pet, Home backgrounds, themes.
4. **Focus:** sound mixer, Brain Wrapped.
