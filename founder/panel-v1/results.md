These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- If the widgets and the Dynamic Island timer were the useful part and cost about $2 to $3 on their own, or if I had been using the app daily for a few weeks and really liked it.
- Seeing App Store reviews and Reddit posts from real ADHD users saying the widgets and Dynamic Island timer actually help them start, or a cheaper widgets-only option around $3.
- If the Dynamic Island timer and widgets actually helped me start tasks and a friend showed me it worked, or if it was a couple dollars.
- If I'd already been using the free app daily for a month and the widget felt genuinely useful, or a price of around $2.99 for just widgets and one pet.
- A much lower price, around $2 to $3, or one extra that actually helps me start tasks, like the routine packs, sold alone.
- If the routine packs and the Dynamic Island timer were sold on their own for about $2-3 and clearly helped me start tasks faster.
- If I'd already been using the free app daily for a month and my pet was something I actually cared about, I might buy a new species for a couple of dollars.
- A much lower price, around $2 to $3, or one cheap pet species I could buy on its own without the rest.
- If I'm still using the free app daily after a month and love my pet, I might buy for the new species. Or if it was around $2-3.
- If I'd still been using it every day for a couple of months and one cute pet or the widget really helped me start tasks, maybe at a lower price.
- If the widgets and Dynamic Island timer were sold alone as a small, clearly useful pack, with real reviews saying they help people actually start tasks.
- A much lower price, around $2 to $3, or if I'd used the free app daily for a few weeks and really loved my pet.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
