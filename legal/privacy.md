# Privacy Policy — Gobblo

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Gobblo ("the app", "we") is made by **Anass Kraifa**, an independent developer in Morocco.
Contact: **[anasskraifa79@gmail.com](mailto:anasskraifa79@gmail.com)**

**The short version:** your tasks, your pet and everything you type stay on your phone. We have no
accounts and no servers that receive them. We can't see them, sell them or lose them in a data breach.
The app sends only anonymous usage counts and crash reports, so we can fix and improve it, and you can
turn that off in one tap (section 2).

## 1. What stays on your device (we never receive it)

- **Your content:** bites (tasks), brain dumps, reminders, your pet's name and look, sparks, focus
  sessions, stats, the optional "good things" notes and all your settings.
- It is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login.
- **No tracking.** Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. Your progress charts are calculated on
  your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is described in section 2 (anonymous counts and
  crash reports), section 5 (ads, Android only) and section 6 (purchases, iPhone only).

## 2. Anonymous usage and crash data

To learn which features help and to fix crashes, the app sends a small amount of anonymous data.
It is **on by default** and you can turn it off at any time in
**Settings › Privacy & data › Share anonymous usage**. When it is off, nothing is sent.

**What is sent**

- A random install ID made by the app. It is not linked to your name, email or any account, and we
  never try to find out who you are.
- Anonymous counts of what happens in the app, for example: the app was opened, how long a visit
  lasted (rounded), which screen was opened (its name only), an onboarding step was reached, a bite
  was added (its size, not its text), a focus timer started or ended (its length, rounded), a bite
  was fed, a level was reached, a tool was opened, a closet item was unlocked, a colour theme was
  picked, a settings switch was changed (which one, not what you typed), a video offer was shown or
  watched (Android), the Plus page was seen, bought or restored (iPhone).
- Basic technical info: app version, operating system and version, device model, the language of
  your phone (2 letters, such as "en"), and your country, which is worked out from your IP address
  by the analytics provider. The IP address itself is not stored.
- Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, the type of error, where in the code it
  happened, the app version, the operating system and the device model.

**What is never sent**

- Anything you type: your bites, brain dumps, reminders, "good things" notes or your pet's name.
- Your name, email, contacts, photos, exact location or advertising ID.
- Screen recordings or screenshots, what you tap on, or what is on your screen.

**Who processes it**

- **PostHog** (usage counts), hosted in the European Union. Policy: <https://posthog.com/privacy>
- **Sentry** (crash reports), EU data region. Policy: <https://sentry.io/privacy/>

They process the data for us and keep it for their standard retention periods (see their policies).
We don't sell this data or use it for ads.

**Why:** to see which features help, where people get stuck, and to fix bugs. Under the GDPR our
legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it, and you can
object at any time with the switch above.

## 3. Backups

- **iPhone:** if iCloud Backup is on, iOS includes the app's data in your encrypted device backup.
- **Android:** if Google backup is on, Android's Auto Backup saves the app's data to your Google
  account and restores it when you reinstall the app.
- These backups are run by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never receive them.
- **Export:** "Export backup" in Settings creates a file that you save wherever you choose. We never
  receive it.

## 4. Phone features the app uses

- **Notifications** are scheduled on your phone (no push server). You choose how often, or turn them off.
- **Face ID / fingerprint lock** (optional) is checked by your phone's system. The app only learns
  "unlocked" or "not unlocked". It never receives your face or fingerprint data.
- **Sounds and vibration:** nothing is recorded. The app never uses the microphone or camera.

## 5. Ads (Android version only)

The Android app is free. Its only ads are optional "watch to earn" videos, shown through
**Google AdMob**, and only when you choose to watch one. There are no banners and no pop-up ads. Ads
never appear in onboarding, during a focus session, while feeding your pet, during an evolution or
in the brain dump.

- AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address (for an
  approximate location), and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads.
- Ads never receive your tasks or anything you type.
- In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can
  change your choice at any time in **Settings › Privacy options**.
- Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (**Settings › Google › Ads**).

**The iPhone and iPad version has no ads and no tracking.**

## 6. Purchases (iPhone and iPad only)

If you buy **Gobblo Plus**, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or your Apple ID.
To know what you have unlocked (and to restore it), the app uses **RevenueCat**. RevenueCat receives
an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information. It does not receive
your name or email. RevenueCat's policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

## 7. Children

Gobblo is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't
knowingly collect any data from them.

## 8. Health

Gobblo is a productivity app made for ADHD brains. It is **not a medical device**. It doesn't
diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

## 9. Your rights

We don't hold your content. The anonymous counts and crash reports in section 2 are not linked to
you, so we can't look them up by person. You can stop them at any time with
**Settings › Privacy & data › Share anonymous usage**. You control your content on your phone:

- **See it:** it's all in the app.
- **Take it with you:** Settings › Export backup.
- **Delete it:** Settings › Reset everything, or delete the app.

For data that Google (ads), RevenueCat (purchases), PostHog or Sentry hold, you can contact them directly or write to
us and we'll help. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or Morocco's Law 09-08 give you
these rights. In Morocco you can also contact the CNDP (<https://www.cndp.ma>).

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
